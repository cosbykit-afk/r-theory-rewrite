The IO-Word Campaign

Book 24. Mass, the dial, and the no-go results — what a month of computations with the 1820's eight IO words established, what they ruled out, and what is still missing.

First published 2026-09-30

About this book. In September 2026 the proving ground ran roughly sixty numbered computations against the 1820's IO-word machinery: the mass dependence of the n̂54 prefactor, the E-contact dial as a measurement instrument, the epsilon sign pattern, and the C8 structure of the candidate space. The campaign's shape turned out to be one corrected law, one working instrument, and a long list of proved negatives. This book is the intuition-first account: about a hundred claims, every one labeled with exactly what the math established. Full derivations live in Appendix P; the complete claim list is ledger §12–§13 on the ledger page.

Status key. §1 — The mass question

The manuscript said a certain n̂54 prefactor scales as m2−4. The campaign's first job was to check whether that exponent could even follow from the stated t-channel content. It cannot: two Me−1 insertions supply exactly degree-2 inverse-mass monomials, and straddling (0,2) transfers force the mixed (m0m2)−1 form. A fit gave the m2 exponent as 1.000000, not 4.

The m2−4 law is falsified. FALSIFIED Runs 44/48. Counting plus exact-block straddle theorems; the discriminator D(3,5)/D(2,5) = 2/3 exactly where the manuscript's content predicted 1.0. (Derivation: Appendix P-5.)

The corrected law. n̂54 = −(1/256)‖QF‖ cord (m0m2)−1 = −(√10/1536) SF (m0m2)−1 for Type-S transfer, while manuscript-pair (Type-R) routing is mass-independent. PROVED Run 48: analytic proof plus numeric tripwire — 20/20 checks (6/6 exact Fraction arithmetic, 14/14 fresh-draw block checks), ratios 2/3 and 5/11 exact.

Routing lemma. For A = Et M Et each transfer leg samples the grade block of M it dips into: Type S (0,2 legs), Type R (E legs), and mixed Type M routings. The mixed dial splits D = DSS + DRR + DSR with dependences (m0m2)−1, mass-independent, and {m0−1,m2−1} respectively; the manuscript's stated E-contact routing is Type R and therefore mass-independent. PROVED Run 51: 35/35 checks (a dense tripwire caught a missing cross-term factor, 2→4, before the pass).

Mass decoupling. [Me(m0,m2,Mrest), X] = [Mrest, X] for every X in {Mε, C1, C2, C3} and all real m0, m2: the masses cancel from every established IO commutator, residuals ≤ 6.9×10−15. PROVED Run 52. (Disclosed: the report says “30 checks” but the results JSON lists 29 check entries — all passing; a naming/counting difference, no failing check.)

The proved negative on selection. No epsilon-sector choice constrains (m0, m2) — not a ratio, not a sign, not even m0 ≠ m2. The avenue “select m0/m2 via the IO-word/flatwave epsilon structure” is closed. FALSIFIED Run 3. (See also Appendix P-2: [Me, Mε] = i [Mrest, K] exactly, for all real m0, m2 — the epsilon-sector-preserving condition imposes nothing on the masses.)

Refinements that survived. The real Pk-algebra supports a five-scalar mass refinement (c0,c4 | c2 | c1,c3), all IO-invisible; the O sector admits a real invisible split P0−P4, but the B sector does not, since P2−P6 is irreducibly non-real. PROVED Run 52. And for mixed X in V, Me X Me = m0(Π0XR + RΠ0) + m2(Π2XR + RΠ2) exactly, with R = Mrest — the grade masses enter only through the H-central projectors. PROVED Runs 60/61, operator-level (T0).

§2 — The dial as instrument

The complete derivations behind the dial theorems used in this campaign are written out in full in Appendix P, §P-3 — the dial theorems — D₈, D_W, D_B and the cross-ratio identity, each with its scope label. Each derivation carries its scope label; the appendix is the authority, this box is the doorway.

The campaign's one working measurement device is the dial: an 8-cycle trace functional that reads out the transfer content of the B–O sector. Think of it as a gauge you bolt onto the 1820 — feed it a transfer matrix Z, and the needle D(Z) tells you whether anything is actually moving between the sectors. The surprise was that the needle has a closed form, and that two needles together can detect something neither sees alone.

The D8 closed form. The ε-odd 8-cycle dial on Et = ΠE + F(Z) equals D8 = ‖ZTZ‖F2 in closed form — nonzero exactly when the B–O transfer content Z is nonzero. PROVED Run 30: 16/16 checks; F(Z) alone gives the identical dial to 10−12 (ΠE-drop invariance). The dressed-O bare-8-cycle dial has the same closed form and is blind to O-flip γ (exactly 0 flip-only), while the W4 and Me−1 dials resolve γ in sign-definite closed forms. PROVED Run 31: 31/31 checks. (Derivations: Appendix P-3.)

The two-needle test. With DW = 2 m0m2 C + m02D8 and DB = 2 (m0m2)−1 C + m0−2D8: bare witnesses give DWDB = D82 exactly; dressed witnesses give DWDB ≥ D82 — a dial test for O-flip content, since the excess over the square comes only from dressed transfer. PROVED (Appendix P-3).

Flip-only Oprop dials. The manuscript-faithful flip-only dials equal (m0m2)±1 times two distinct nonnegative transfer functionals, each vanishing when either transfer vanishes. PROVED Run 33: 48/48 checks. (Disclosed: the run log ends with a NameError on the final sys.exit line — a missing import, after all 48 checks had passed and the results JSON was written.)

Mode channels {0,4}. The flip-dial core splits into exact C8 mode channels U′ = U′[0] + U′[4] and V = V[0] + V[4], with only channels {0,4} existing, and the transpose-cyclicity corollary U′ = V holds for all real flips. PROVED Run 34: 27/27 checks. The [4]-channel is dial-visible destructive cross-mode interference. The pair-restricted dressed-O dial is γ-resolvable exactly when F engages at least one Π0-side and one Π2-side contact pair — one-sided transfer gives an identically dead SF dial for every Oprop flip. PROVED Run 42: 13/13 checks.

§3 — The no-go results

The campaign kept asking “can the 1820's own machinery select, resolve, or build this object?” and the mathematics kept answering no, in closed form. A proved negative is a finished piece of work: it tells the next campaign where not to dig.

Bilinear rigidity. Every nonzero real combination F of the 64 mixed bilinears carries exactly the per-pair weight pattern (221, 117, 25, 1, 1, 25, 117, 221). Uniform E-contact bridges are impossible — and cannot even be approached. PROVED Runs 47/50: 5/5 checks, H-commutant argument gives Qp = patternp I64, residuals ≤ 1.2×10−11. The run-50 fork extends the rigidity to complex and Hermitian combinations (all eight imaginary weight matrices vanish, max norm 5.4×10−12). CHECKED Run 54: 7/7.

The uniform-1/4 bridge, as a bilinear witness, is falsified. No single bilinear witness — and no C8/C4 symmetrization of the mixed supply — can realize it; the only g2-invariant element of the mixed span is 0. FALSIFIED Run 47. What is not touched: the 1/4 value itself. Only the uniformity was tested; the per-contact value stays ASSERTED.

The selection census. For all 12 bilinear-odd reps, ‖[X̃, Mε]‖F2 = 752 = 4·188, the real commutator Gram is 752·I12, and the flip-slot kernel is {0}. PROVED Runs 21–23: 17/17 checks (this corrects run 21's K2 — the 6-dim kernel was a basis-mixing artifact, withdrawn 2026-09-27; see Appendix P-5). The Mε commutant over ℂ is the 1,673,872-dim block algebra M816(ℂ) + M1004(ℂ) — over ℝ, 845,968-dim — with all four transfer slots and the Π0↔Π2 slot equal to {0}. PROVED Run 23. And the bottom line: no established operator selects a vector or proper subspace inside the real 12-dim bilinear-odd space b. CHECKED Run 23: complete census, 26/28 (the 2 FAILs were falsified avenues, not gaps). The census extends to the 64-dim mixed E-contact supply V: preserving maps act only as scalar pairs, and the zero maps include the C8 average and the grade-projector conjugations. CHECKED Run 59: 22/22 + 9/9.

Every real X in b has w2 = w6 exactly — no nonzero real mode-pure element exists; run 20's mode-pure splitting holds only on the complexification. PROVED Run 20 correction (Appendix P-2).

The SF dial does not resolve mode charge. That hypothesis is falsified: |U′±| > |U′++| on all 60/60 (Z,γ) pairs, and S(Z) is full rank 12 on every draw — the dial mixes modes and selects nothing. FALSIFIED Run 57, within the run-33/34 candidate framework. The dial quadratic form is spectrally blind to the C8 action: S(AdgZ) = M S(Z) MT, and for i.i.d. Gaussian Z, E[S(Z)] = 2·204610·94·I12 exactly. PROVED Run 57: 12/12 checks.

Word combinatorics. Every cyclic word over {E, B, O, Me} reduces to scalar sector arithmetic: of all 87,380 length-1–8 words, 85,866 are exactly zero and the 1,514 nonzero fall in 32 classes with no intra-class mismatches. PROVED Run 25 (Appendix P-5). The 8-cycle is exactly zero with bare witnesses, with dressed Et alone, and with Me−1 plus bilinear O flip — so any Ek making it nonzero must contain E↔{B,O} transfer content: a requirement bound on the missing contact operator, not a manuscript derivation. PROVED Run 25.

The Tier-1 embedding is dead. No nonzero SO(16)-equivariant map exists from the 120 (adjoint) into Sym2(128), Sym20(128), or the 1820 = V(ω4) — and 1820 ≠ Sym20(128) (8255 ≠ 1820). The manuscript §19.4's N1820 8-cycle is dead with it: EBEOEBEO = 0 since Πrest·Π0 = 0, ‖·‖F = 1.992×10−61, 24/24 checks.

§4 — Epsilon and C8

Two structural questions ran through the whole campaign: is the epsilon sign pattern unique, and what does the C8 symmetry actually do to the candidate space? Both got definitive answers — the pattern is not unique, and the C8 action is rigidly structured — and one of the answers turned out to be stronger than the question asked.

The epsilon pattern is not unique. Exactly 2 canonical sign patterns carry the full 120-channel signature: the adopted pattern and its C1-multiple — the doubly-alternating family. PROVED Run 7: Lemmas A/B/C plus CHECKED converse, 22/22 checks. But the ambiguity is harmless where it matters: for every real observable T, the ε+ sector spectrum is identical under both survivors — o-spectral-invisibility, a proved negative stronger than the question asked. PROVED Run 17: 30/30 checks (Appendix P-2).

Mε structure. Mε = Re(Mε) + i Im(Mε) with Re(Mε) = Π0 − Π2 exactly, and K = Im(Mε) a complex structure on the 896-dim Πrest (KT = −K, K2 = −Πrest); the epsilon sectors are the ±i eigenspaces of K. PROVED 2026-09-25, residuals ~10−15. The claim that an exact real Z2 reproduces the epsilon sectors is falsified: ‖Πε+ − conj(Πε+)‖F = 29.93. FALSIFIED 2026-09-25.

Sign content, demoted to a choice. All 16 IO-word epsilon analogs (g-power M(j), additive rotations E(j), multiplicative permutations F(u)) are doubly-alternating, collapse to 4 patterns, and carry the same channel signature on all 120 generators. PROVED 2026-09-26. The eight IO words at octant midpoints span exactly the 4-dim sign subalgebra {1, s1, s2, ε}, and the flatwave eps sign is derived as −s1·s2 up to a global sign. PROVED Run 32: 17/17 — the eps pattern is demoted from definitional input to a global-sign choice. And Mε = −C1·C2 as operators; under the adopted octant indexing Mε = F1820 with no −C1 factor (the earlier −C1 was an off-by-one artifact). PROVED + CHECKED (1.92×10−15) Runs 32/36.

C8 adjoint structure. The 12-dim Oprop ansatz space b carries six copies of the charge-2 real C8 irrep: Adg is block-diagonal over the (dpq, opq) pair-planes. PROVED Run 55: 22/22 checks. The octagon average (1/8)∑Adgj annihilates b; the charge projectors split b into ±i halves with the support rules +i:{(2,0),(6,4)}, −i:{(6,0),(2,4)} plus reverses. PROVED Run 55: 22/22, mode purity 12/12. dimℝ b = 12 exactly, with Gram 188·I12 and f02 = 47/182 for every rot8-cross generator. PROVED Runs 14–15 (Appendix P-2).

The y2 angular factor. cos(4x) has the exact IO-word form 2[(srx+crx−sxp−cxp)/(uxp+urx)]2 − 1, vanishing at all 8 IO-word octant midpoints — so the y2 factor is identically zero at every octant-table sample point. PROVED Run 49: 13/13, residuals ≤ 3.1×10−15 on 3958 grid points. And the manuscript's “vanishes at the octant boundaries” is exactly backwards: cos(4x) = (−1)k at the boundaries 45°·k (maxima). PROVED Run 49. The asserted eps pattern factors exactly as εk = −(−1)k·FWk. PROVED Run Q4 (exact integer arithmetic).

Epsilon on the 54. E135 commutes with P54; the 54 splits 28(+)/26(−) under epsilon, with real content −1×18, +1×20 and 16 phase-only odd-mode dims; the U-23 target direction T̃ is epsilon-minus-pure. PROVED Run 58: 16/16 checks. The sweep eps pattern [−1,+1,+1,−1,−1,+1,+1,−1] reproduces the manuscript's Book-17 epsilon column 8/8 under j → k = j mod 8 (E1 = +1 pins the adopted pattern), closing the pattern-level o-ambiguity for manuscript-faithful work. CHECKED Run 56: 8/8. The 120-channel: Mε normalizes the unique equivariant antisymmetric 120-channel on 88/120 generators (group adjoint Adρ(g)−2), expelling the 64 mixed generators exactly into imaginary-symmetric space orthogonal to the real channel. PROVED Run 2.

§5 — The run-61 correction

Science includes the part where you find out you were wrong, say so in writing, and keep what survives. Runs 59 and 60 had reported that the Me-conjugation compresses to scalar pairs — clean numbers, surprising ones. Run 61 recomputed everything densely, twice, by independent methods. The scalars did not survive. What follows is the correction, with the numbers that replaced them.

Run 59's scalar pair: withdrawn. The “compression of Me-conjugation is exactly the scalar pair (9.570537561505088, 9.570537561505088)” was overstated: dense recompute gives trace/64 = +9.570537561505086 (the diagonal matches) but ‖off-diag‖F = 0.1657 — not scalar. The 4.6×10−15 was only the W↔W̄ mixing blocks; within-block |A − λPW| = 0.1184. FALSIFIED Runs 59/61.

Run 60's piece-compressions: withdrawn. They are not scalar pairs either: ‖off-diag‖F = 0.0241 (C0), 0.0267 (C2), two independent methods agreeing — and run 60's “corollaries” of scalar linearity λ(m0,m2) = m0λ0 + m2λ2 as a scalar identity are withdrawn with them. FALSIFIED Runs 60/61. (The run-60 “triplet index bug” was a misdiagnosis: dense-sandwich and triplet-einsum agree to 10−12 on individual entries, 5/5 spot checks. CHECKED Run 61.)

What survives. T0 holds at the compression level: ‖SVMe − (2·SV0 + 5·SV2)‖F = 2.0×10−14 — the operator identity implies the compression identity exactly; no contradiction. CHECKED Run 61 (scope: T0 PROVED by block algebra, plus operator tripwire 4×10−15 and compression 2.0×10−14). The revised picture: SVMe = 9.570537561505086·I64 + E, E real symmetric, traceless (3.9×10−14), ‖E‖F = 0.1675, preserving the W/W̄ charge split (|EB|,|EC| ~ 3×10−15) while non-scalar within blocks — with E = 2N0 + 5N2. CHECKED Run 61, preliminary. The signs correct too: λ0 = +0.974753893412292, λ2 = +1.524205954936101 — positive; the stage-1 log's negatives came from a script edited after the run. CHECKED Run 61. What run 59 measured correctly stays: W↔W̄ mixing = 0 (re-verified at 3.3×10−15), the compression trace, and Me's non-centrality ([Me,ρ(h)] ~ 14.3 and 18.8). CHECKED Run 61.

One question dissolved. Run 60's open question — “why do the piece maps compress to scalar pairs?” — is dissolved: the premise was false. Do not pursue it. The mechanism of E's charge-split-respecting non-scalar structure remains open, but it is no longer a “surprise.”

§6 — Open gates: what the campaign did not finish

The campaign built the instrument and mapped where it cannot go. The target is still missing. Everything below is open — stated here so the next campaign starts from the true boundary, not from anyone's hopes.

Open.

  • Physical Oprop. The actual Oprop matrix is unselected inside the exact 12-dim candidate space b; physical selection needs physical input.
  • Physical Ek contact operators. Unconstructed. The manuscript's explicit Ek form is open, and any Ek that revives the 8-cycle must contain E↔{B,O} transfer content (the run-25 requirement bound).
  • Physical C8 action. The adopted g is not the physical action; this stays open and is inherited by all ρ(g)-derived statements.
  • SF. Definition adjudication and numerical extraction remain open; extraction needs the physical witnesses.
  • m0/m2 selection. Provably unstatable in IO-word terms (§1); the dial identification via the D8/DW/DB ratios is the unidentified path. The archived m0 = 2, m2 = 5 are reference values — a placeholder, not a physical selection.
  • N1820. Unresolved: the 8-cycle is dead as its probe, and the B/O per-factors β, ω and the absorbed witness norm remain manuscript-undefined.
  • The 1/4 per-contact value stays ASSERTED — only its uniformity as a bilinear witness was tested.
  • FC8 is absent, so the U-23 falsification verdict stays FC8-gated; the t-channel contraction Ct2222 is unexecutable.
  • The Gr(10,16) physical ten-plane stays deliberately open.
  • The 29 GATEs (GATE-8..GATE-36) with precise crossing criteria live in the research pages, §7.

Standing. Nothing in this book upgrades a CHECKED claim to PROVED, an ASSERTED value to a selection, or a proved negative to a positive. The instrument is calibrated; the target is absent. That is the honest boundary the campaign reached — and the next campaign starts there.
