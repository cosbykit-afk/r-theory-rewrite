Appendix P — Full Derivations

R Theory Rewrite Series — the complete written-out proofs behind the series' established results.

What this appendix is. The series' book pages and the research status ledger publish verdicts: PROVED, CHECKED, REFUTED, and the rest. This appendix publishes the derivations — the full proofs, the exact algebra, and the negative results worked out step by step — for readers who want to check the work rather than take the verdict. It is long on purpose: a webpage costs nothing to lengthen, and a proof costs everything to shorten.

Scope discipline. Every derivation carries its scope label, stated up front and never rounded up or down: checked proof completed symbolic check completed numerical check incorrect result (refutations). Premises are stated before the proofs that need them. Where a source document has a genuine gap, the gap is labeled in place — this appendix invents no mathematics.

Not the audit trail. The Computational Audit Appendix records how the audit was run (modules, scripts, run logs). This appendix records what was proved.

Coverage. P-1–P-6 cover the 2026-09-21…28 proof burst (Casimir, the 1820 campaign, the dials, the rebuilt modules, the refutations, two standalone theorems). P-7 gives a condensed derivation paragraph for each of the 86 Books-2–6 audit claims in the ledger; where the ledger's verdict is too thin for a paragraph, the entry is an explicitly labeled pointer, not a proof. P-8 is the reproducibility index: every validation script, what it establishes, and its last known run result. The final section lists what is still open — honestly, and never presented as closed.
