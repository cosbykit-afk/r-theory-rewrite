Run 25: the manuscript's N₁₈₂₀ 8-cycle is dead incorrect result

Claim under test. The probe. Extended to the full cyclic-word algebra over {E, B, O, Me} (Me = archived reference).

FALSIFIED (clean): the 8-cycle operator is exactly zero. The product

EBEOEBEO = Π_rest · Π_0 · Π_rest · Π_2 · Π_rest · Π_0 · Π_rest · Π_2

contains the factor Πrest·Π0 = 0, so the operator vanishes identically — not just its trace. Checked on archived matrices: ‖EBEOEBEO‖F = 1.99e-61, |Tr| = 6.10e-61, all 8 cyclic adjacent-pair junctions ≤ 2.085e-14. Stronger form (exact combinatorics): the 8-cycle has pairwise-distinct letters at every adjacent pair, so this holds for any bijection of {E,B,O} onto {Π0,Π2,Πrest} — it does not depend on the run-4b letter map at all.

With the charitable formula N = ccontact⁴·β²·ω²·Tr(EBEOEBEO)/Wnorm, the result is 0 for every choice of the unspecified B/O per-factors β, ω and every nonzero witness norm W — the undefined inputs are moot; the zero is exact and independent of them.

PROVED (exact algebra) + CHECKED: full cyclic-word classification. Premises re-verified on archived matrices (all ≤ 1.6e-13): the ΠI symmetric idempotents, pairwise orthogonal, complete, traces 368/556/896; [Me, ΠI] = 0; Π0MeΠ0 = 2Π0; Π2MeΠ2 = 5Π2. Since every letter is block-diagonal in the grade-sector basis, every cyclic word over {E,B,O,Me} reduces to scalar sector arithmetic:

Tr(w) = 368 · 2^k · [w ∈ {B,M}*] + 556 · 5^k · [w ∈ {O,M}*] + T_k · [w ∈ {E,M}*],

with k = #(M in w) and Tk = Tr(Πrest·Mek). A word is nonzero iff its non-M letters are all the same witness (or absent). Checked: exhaustive enumeration of all 87,380 words of length 1–8 — 85,866 exactly zero, 1,514 nonzero in 32 classes, zero intra-class mismatches; 22 dense spot-checks agree with the formula to 3.3e-13. The entire cyclic-word trace algebra is the sector census {368, 556, 896} plus {Tk}: no graph invariant can be isolated from cyclic words over established inputs. Even the nonzero constant words (Tr E⁸ = 896, B⁸ = 368, O⁸ = 556 — the sector dims themselves) cannot be normalized to a discrete graph invariant without the norm the manuscript leaves undefined.

Net. The §19.4 "8-cycle isolates N₁₈₂₀ as an exact discrete graph invariant" route is closed by falsification on established inputs — the first matrix-level test of that passage. N₁₈₂₀ itself stays OPEN: the probe gives a proved reason the 8-cycle cannot supply it (the operator is 0; the word algebra is sector-spectral data only), not a value for it.

Gap: the B/O per-factors β, ω and the "absorbed witness norm" remain manuscript-undefined (MA); the probe shows they cannot rescue the construction, not what their values are. The non-operator ("counting") reading of §19.4 has no evaluable content on established inputs — noted, not tested further.

The withdrawn 12 → 6 reduction: a basis-mismatch bug incorrect result

What run 21 claimed (2026-09-27 ~01:25 PDT). b_meps_commutant.py reported: all six o-reps commute with Meps to 4.58e-16; all six d-reps have ‖[d,Meps]‖²F = 752.0000000000 = 4·188 uniformly; commutator Gram spectrum {0 ×6, 752 ×6}; hence ker([·,Meps]) inside the real 12-dim bilinear-odd space b is the 6-dim o-half — a conditional 12 → 6 selection principle for the Oprop search.

The bug. The script built the 12 rep dicts in the u-wedge basis (Xu = U†X₁₆U, then the wedge differential — basis = Λ⁴ of the u-basis, charges {0..7}), then loaded the archived Meps1820.npy, which is expressed in the standard-wedge (e) basis (lexicographic ei₁∧ei₂∧ei₃∧ei₄; verified monomial), and commuted the two without any basis change. The two bases differ by Λ⁴(U). The sparse "commutator" run 21 computed is not the commutator of the two operators — it mixes bases.

How it was caught. Run 22 (diag_rerun21.py) reran run 21's exact pipeline and reproduced its numbers verbatim (6 × 4.58e-16, 6 × 752.0000000000) — confirming the old output is reproducible but basis-inconsistent. Independent code reading (Q1_752_PROOF.md) confirmed: build_D constructs rep dicts in the u-wedge basis, then sp_comm multiplies them directly against the e-basis archived Meps with no Λ⁴(U) transform.

Corrected result — PROVED (block algebra, basis-free). Meps = diag(+I₃₆₈, −I₅₅₆, iKr) in the (Π0,Π2,Πrest) split (premises checked ≤ 2.05e-15). For any pure Π0↔Π2 flip X̃ = Π0DΠ2 + Π2DΠ0 (real or complex),

[X̃, M_eps] = [[0, -2A], [2B, 0]]   (rest blocks zero),
||[X̃, M_eps]||_F = 2 ||X̃||_F,

so [X̃,Meps] = 0 implies A = B = 0, i.e. X̃ = 0. ker([·,Meps]) on the entire pure Π0↔Π2 slot is {0} — its intersection with the 12-dim space b has dimension 0, not 6. Checked in two bases: all 12 u-basis reps give ‖[B,Meps]‖²F = 752 = 4·188 exactly, no d/o split; on 6 archived real flips ‖[X̃,Meps]‖ = 2‖X̃‖ to rel 2.59e-16 — never zero.

Net. Run 21's K2 is falsified and superseded; the "6-dim o-half commutant" and the conditional 12 → 6 Meps-commutant selection principle are withdrawn. The bug was a basis mismatch, not a near-miss: the computation never evaluated the claimed commutator. Run 21's K1 (reality theorem, below) is unaffected — it used only u-basis quantities. The corrected law is stronger and simpler than run 21's: a uniform exact identity (norm exactly doubles) replacing a spurious 6/6 split. A commutator-based (firewall-preserving) selection inside the pure (0,2)-slot selects nothing.

Gap: the commutator Gram spectrum argument is withdrawn with K2; the analytic proof of the uniform 752 = 4·188 d-norms now follows from the block identity, but the original "combinatorial proof via the wedge-permutation action" remains future work if desired.
