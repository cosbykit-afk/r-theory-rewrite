P-4-m8. M8′ — corrected congruence-action Casimir C′

checked proof

2026-09-25; modules7_12_replacements.md §M8′

Primary definition.

C′(M) = −Σa (Aa²M + 2AaMAaT + M(AaT)²)

Claim 1. C′ is the Casimir operator of the congruence action g·M = gMgT on Sym²(128). PROVED (independent): the infinitesimal action is ρ(A)M = AM + MAT, and ρ(A)²M = A(AM+MAT) + (AM+MAT)AT = A²M + 2AMAT + M(AT)² by exact matrix algebra (ledger B-4 addendum).

Claim 2 (B-5 rehabilitation). In the exhibited real basis (Frobenius–Schur +1; real structure J with J²=+1, [J,Aa]=0; 120 generators real antisymmetric to ∼1e-15), the manuscript's encoded formula satisfies Rmanuscript(M) = −C′(M). CHECKED (numeric): worst relative ‖R+C′‖/‖C′‖ = 5.4e-16 over 6 trials (b5_reality_check.py, CLOSED 2026-09-23). The manuscript's formula is thus rehabilitated up to the overall sign, in the real basis; in the manuscript's (complex) basis it remains the wrong operator.

Claim 3. C′ is central: [C′, ρ(A)] = 0 for all generators. Standard Lie theory (a representation's Casimir commutes with the representation) + CHECKED numeric: max |[C′,ρ]| ≈ 2.8–2.9e-14 on random symmetric matrices (diag_casimir.py §3).

Diagnosis of the original. The encoded R(M) = Σa[Aa,[Aa,M]] is ρ(A)² for ρ(A)M = [A,M] — the Casimir of the conjugation action g·M = gMg−1.
