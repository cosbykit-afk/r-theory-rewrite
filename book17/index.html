§17.1 — Primitive octant decomposition (chunk 1)

17.1.1 harmonic carrier. On every quadrant, srx − 1/srx = 2cos x/sin x, cxp − 1/cxp = 2sin x/cos x, hence urx + uxp = 2/(sin x cos x) = 4/sin(2x) and sin(2x)/4 = 1/(urx+uxp). CP (dense numeric tripwire: max relative error 3.3e-14 over 60,001 grid points NC). 17.1.2 imbalance. K₂ = 2cos(2x)/(sin x cos x), D₂ = 2/(sin x cos x), K₂/(2D₂) = cos(2x)/2. CP 17.1.3 elliptic carrier. V_R² + 4H² = 1/4 and the stub-proof route D₂² − K₂² = 16. CP 17.1.4 octant table. The dominant-ordering formulas per octant pair — e.g. octants 1–2: srx − cxp = (cos x − sin x)(1 + cos x + sin x)/(sin x cos x) — and all eight per-octant derivative signs are exact. CP Active pair. Via t = tan(x/2), on each open octant exactly the two named active primitives exceed 1 and the other two lie in (0,1); e.g. octant 1: srx − 1 = (1−t)/t > 0, cxp − 1 = 2t/(1−t) > 0 for t ∈ (0, √2−1). CP 17.1.5–6 three-bit code. The eight codes are distinct and cover ℤ₂³; each octant decodes from (half-quadrant, sgn cos 2x, sgn cos x). CP

§17.2 — The 2:1 elliptic map (chunk 1)

V_R(x+π) = V_R(x), H(x+π) = H(x). The eight octant midpoints land on four ellipse points (±√2/4, ±√2/8), concyclic at distance √10/8; boundary points (±1/2, 0), (0, ±1/4) at x = 0°, 45°, 90°, 135°, 180°. CP The deck map x → x+π fixes ε = sgn(sin 2x) — the IC-1 exhibit. CP

Part III — Incorrect, incomplete, and open

Incorrect as stated (this book: IC-1…IC-11)

  • IC-1 (§17.2): ε = sgn(sin 2x) does not record the 2:1 sheet — it is invariant under the deck map x → x+π. It records ellipse halves. IC
  • IC-2 (§17.3.5): the printed radical for crx(112.5°) evaluates ≈ 0.6682, not ≈ 5.027. Correct: 1/cxp(112.5°) = √(4+2√2)+1+√2 ≈ 5.0273. IC
  • IC-3 (§17.1.4): 1/(urx·uxp) is the reciprocal of the product, not of the geometric mean. IC
  • IC-4 (§17.6.2): "cos(4x) vanishes at the octant boundaries" is false — it vanishes at the octant midpoints (22.5° + k·45°); at the boundaries it is ±1. Figure 4 is the exhibit. IC
  • IC-5 (§17.18): "all four O propagators" — the C8 word contains exactly two O's. IC
  • IC-6 (§17.18): "the 120 sits inside Sym²(128)" — false; the 120 lives in Λ²(128) = 120 ⊕ 8008, while Sym²(128) = 1 ⊕ 1820 ⊕ 6435 has no 120. IC
  • IC-7 (§17.18): "the 135 is the adjoint of SO(16)" — false; the adjoint has dimension 16·15/2 = 120. The 135 is Sym²₀(16), the traceless symmetric square of the vector. IC
  • IC-8 (skeleton Step 1): NullSpace[Transpose[{traceVector}]] as written yields {} (rank 1 ⇒ nullity 0); the intended reading yields an 8255-dimensional space, not 1820. The correct construction needs P₆₄₃₅, which v1 says is not in the authoritative documents — unfixable within v1. IC
  • IC-9 (skeleton Steps 2–3): the 128⁴×128⁴ Kronecker product (268,435,456 × 268,435,456) cannot be sandwiched by an 1820×8256 basis — dimensionally incompatible as written. IC
  • IC-10 (skeleton Steps 6–7): −(1/256) is double-counted — Step 6 dresses the stripped S_F, and Step 7's −(√10/1536) = −(1/256)(√10/6) presumes a stripped input, violating §17.18.10's own "counted exactly once" regression requirement. IC
  • IC-11 (skeleton Step 5): wickSign is never assigned anywhere in the skeleton. IC
