Volume 0 — Exact Witnesses at the Physics Boundary

The former T4–T6 "Projection Craft" papers, rebuilt as standalone R Theory.

What this volume is. Three books that take the canonical half-angle calculus of Book 0 to the boundary of accepted physics — relativistic kinematics, the Dirac spinor, weak helicity, Coulomb structure, color, bound states, and canonical spin–geometry — and report exactly what is found there: exact coordinate correspondences, exact conditional theorems, honest obstructions, and no new physical law.

Why it is standalone. The source material was written as "Projection Craft" essay-books T4, T5, T6, framed as continuations of the T-paper series. That framing is not carried over. Each book here states its own dependencies, its own imports, and its own status ledger from scratch. Nothing is cited to a T-paper authority; every result is presented with the mathematics or the import that supports it.

Dependencies. All three books inherit the Book 0 core: the reciprocal primitive calculus, the one-generator reduction, the Clifford/bilinear structure, and the coframe geometry. Books 21 and 22 additionally depend on the internal paper Reciprocal Half-Angle Coordinates in the Radial Dirac–Coulomb Problem (read and algebra-checked 2026-09-19), which supplies the conditional spinor-ratio identities both books use. Named physics imports — special relativity, the Dirac equation, V−A, QCD, Coulomb/Dirac–Coulomb theory, Maxwell, Einstein–Hilbert, Einstein–Cartan — are declared per section, never smuggled.

Provenance note (2026-09-19). The T6 source preamble credits the T5 source with a "Yang–Mills source-free-vacuum obstruction" result — a static homogeneous constant noncommuting ansatz whose curvature the source-free Yang–Mills equations force to vanish. A full-text search of the T5 source (v2 reconstruction) finds no such result: the only occurrence of "Yang–Mills" is the QCD import line. If the original computation is located and verified, it will be added with its source cited; until then the claim does not exist in R Theory.

The shared discipline

Every book in this volume closes with an operational prediction-difference audit. For a fully specified Projection-augmented model MP against its accepted reference M0, on a common measurement contract,

Δ_op(MP, M0) = { c ∈ C_exp : P_P(·|c) ≠ P_0(·|c) }

is the set of experimental configurations where the predictions differ. Three terminal statuses are distinguished: Class A — fully specified and Δ_op = ∅ (operationally equivalent); Class B — incomplete, Δ_op undefined (an open ansatz is not a nonempty prediction set); Class C — fully specified with Δ_op ≠ ∅. An invertible reparameterization can never leave Class A; that is proved, not assumed.

The null hypothesis retained by all three books is C0: Projection Craft changes representation but not physics. It is falsifiable — a future fully specified Class-C model would defeat it — but undefeated by everything in this volume. Exactness without promotion is the theme: the correspondences are real mathematics, and they are not new laws of nature.
