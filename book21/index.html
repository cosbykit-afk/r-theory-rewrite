§1 — The composition firewall

Before color, mass, or binding: what does it mean to compose two systems? For real finite-dimensional carriers there is an exact decomposition

Sym(V_A⊗V_B) = [Sym(V_A)⊗Sym(V_B)] ⊕ [Alt(V_A)⊗Alt(V_B)]

— the locally symmetric product span is not generically the whole composite symmetric sector. A globally symmetric sector (Alt⊗Alt) can be invisible to any reconstruction restricted to local symmetric factors. Consequence, kept as a standing obstruction: a list of locally visible subsystem coordinates cannot be assumed to determine the full composite state unless local tomography is separately established. No later success in this book may be used to erase this rank obstruction.
