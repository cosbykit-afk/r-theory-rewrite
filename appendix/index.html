Appendix: Computational Audit Trail

The exact-script audit of the manuscript's 12-module Wolfram computation (gamma matrices → so(16) generators → Casimir decomposition), its failures as encoded, the corrected constructions, and the independent re-verification. Every claim below carries the status the work actually earned it.

Status vocabulary

This appendix uses the audit's own scope labels, mapped to the series tags: CP checked proof = PROVED · SC completed symbolic check and NC completed numerical check = CHECKED · MA manuscript assertion = ASSERTED · IC incorrect result = INVALID · IN incomplete or failed computation = INCOMPLETE. ST marks standard imported theorems. Nothing is promoted beyond what the mathematics supports: in particular, a numerical check of a corrected construction does not repair the original encoding it replaces.

Method

Two separate computations, written and run independently:

  1. Exact-script audit (NC): run_modules.py, a faithful NumPy/SciPy port of the manuscript's 12 Wolfram modules, run 2026-09-18/19 (~523 s, completed with ALL MODULES DONE; full transcript in run_fixed.log, machine-readable results in results.json). Its purpose was to evaluate the exact script — to find where it breaks, not to confirm it.
  2. Independent re-verification (NC): verify_independent.py, written fresh from the definitions (not a re-run of the audit port), completed 2026-09-21 (~603 s, exit 0). It rebuilds the gamma matrices, the guarded so(16) generators, and the corrected Casimir from scratch, cross-checking the assembled operator entry-by-entry against the literal operator definition on random symmetric matrices.

A third run, diag_casimir.py (2026-09-19, exit 0, ~36 min), ran exhaustive diagnostics on the corrected construction: odd-subspace invariance, all so(16) commutators, and Casimir centrality.

Why Modules 8–12 fail. The encoded double-commutator Σa[Aa,[Aa,M]] is the Casimir only in a real antisymmetric basis. The script's generators are complex anti-Hermitian and act on symmetric matrices by congruence, ρ(A)M = AM + MAT — for which the double commutator is the wrong operator. This is a basis/encoding defect, not numerical noise.

The corrected operator

Expanding ρ(A)² with ρ(A)M = AM + MAT gives the exact algebra (CP by inspection):

C′(M) = −Σa(Aa²M + 2AaMAaT + M(AaT)²)

  • First assembly: spectrum {0:1, 48:1820, 64:6307, 128:128} — IC: the assembly double-counted diagonal (p=q) Sym² basis inputs, producing a spurious 128-eigenspace. Disclosed and patched (p=q handled once).
  • Patched rerun (2026-09-19): distinct eigenvalues {0.0: 1, 48.0: 1820, 64.0: 6435}; eigenvalue range 1.15e-13…64.00000000000173; max|imag| = 0.0; matrix symmetry error 0.0. NC
  • Exhaustive diagnostics (diag_casimir.py): the 128-dim odd subspace is exactly invariant under every even product ΓᵢΓⱼ (max 0.0); all so(16) commutators hold (max violation 0.0); the corrected Casimir is central to machine precision (≈2.8–2.9e-14 against scales ~129–171). NC

Scope. This validates the spectrum of the corrected operator. It does not validate the manuscript's original Modules 8–12 encoding, which remains IC as written.

Analytic status: the spectrum is a theorem

The decomposition is CP (ST/SC components cited), proved 2026-09-19 (K-1…K-9):

  • S₊ (dim 128) carries the chiral spinor representation V(ω₈) of so(16); MAB = ½γAγB satisfy the so(16) relations (K-1, K-2 — hand case analysis on the Clifford relations).
  • C = −Σρ(MAB)² is central (K-2 corollary); the B₊-bilinear singlet is annihilated, Cs = 0 (K-3, K-4).
  • The 1820 B-mapped four-forms span a nonzero submodule F ≅ V(ω₄), dim 1820 (K-5, K-6; the 2-fold terms cancel in [MAB,γI]).
  • V(2ω₈) ⊆ Sym²(S₊), dim 6435 by the Weyl formula — exact symbolic computation (K-7, SC).
  • Sym²(128) = ℂs ⊕ F ⊕ V(2ω₈): 1 + 1820 + 6435 = 8256, direct and exhaustive (K-8).
  • Eigenvalues by Harish-Chandra proportionality with κ pinned on S₊ (−ΣMAB² = 30I) and D₈ arithmetic: ω₈ → 30, ω₄ → 48, 2ω₈ → 64 (K-9). Hence C acts as 0 / 48 / 64 on the three summands.

The corrected operator C′ above is exactly the matrix form of −Σρ(MAB)² on Sym²(S₊) (the vec identity turns (A⊗I+I⊗A)² into A²M + 2AMAT + M(AT)²), so K-9 covers the corrected operator's spectrum. The numerics are corroboration, not the establishment.

What this audit does not establish

  • The manuscript's original Modules 6 and 8–12 as encoded do not establish their claims. The decomposition {0:1, 48:1820, 64:6435} is established for the corrected operator (numerically) and by the analytic theorem — never by the original encoding.
  • Nothing in these checks selects a physical chirality, a preferred lift, or a physical spacetime — consistent with the manuscript's own nonselection theorems.
  • The audit covers only the 12-module computation. The Layer-0/Layer-1 bottleneck is largely unaffected: Oprop, the C8 contraction, and S_F remain absent or mocked, and the dependency chain records them as missing inputs, not missing computation.
