E8 weight table — the 30380

The full weight/multiplicity table for the E8 irreducible representation of dimension 30380, highest Dynkin label (0,0,0,0,0,0,1,0), Bourbaki labeling (chain 1-3-4-5-6-7-8, node 2 on 4). Computed by exact big-integer Freudenthal recursion.

  • table_30380_full.csv — 9,121 distinct weights, multiplicities summing to exactly 30380
  • table_30380_dominant.csv — the 4 dominant weights
  • table_30380_summary.json — summary: histogram, orbit sizes, labeling

Status SC: tripwires passed at every Freudenthal step (exact divisibility and positivity), Weyl dimension formula = 30380, dominant enumeration complete by box bound bA⁻¹bᵀ ≤ (λ,λ) = 6. Dominant multiplicities: λ→1, (1,0,0,0,0,0,0,0)→7, (0,0,0,0,0,0,0,1)→35, 0→140; orbit sizes 6720/2160/240/1 independently re-verified via |W|/|Wstab| (stabilizers E6×A1, D7, E7, E8). Two failed constructions along the way are recorded in the audit log and establish nothing.
