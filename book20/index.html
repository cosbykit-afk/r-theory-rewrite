§1 — The relativistic kinematic witness

Take a massive particle moving at speed β. There is a classical angle hiding in its kinematics: the angle χ with sin χ = β and cos χ = mc²/E. On this chart the half-angle coordinate q = sxp(χ) = tan(χ/2) is simultaneously three familiar things: the free-Dirac lower/upper spinor component ratio, the rational generator of β and γ, and the square root of the light-cone ratio. The reciprocal pair cxp = e^η, crx = e^−η is the rapidity itself — and cxp·crx = 1 is exactly the normalized mass shell, already imposed by the reference theory.

Kinematic dictionary CONDITIONAL

Under the contract sin χ = β, cos χ = mc²/E on the positive-energy branch:

sxp(χ) = pc/(E+mc²) = tan(χ/2),   srx(χ) = (E+mc²)/pc

cxp(χ) = (E+pc)/mc² = e^η,   crx(χ) = (E−pc)/mc² = e^−η

The mapping is invertible on the declared branch; the reciprocal identity is the mass shell. This is a coordinate theorem after importing special-relativistic kinematics and the free Dirac spinor — IMPORT both.

OBSTRUCTION Rank is not created by notation. Evaluating many functions on the single coordinate χ does not manufacture additional independent physical degrees of freedom. The dictionary above is one angle's worth of content, however many names it carries.

§2 — Spinor completion and the native ellipse

One real coordinate is not a spinor. Lifting q = sxp(χ) to a complete two-state spinor requires an independent relative phase φ — the one-generator obstruction survives physical completion. The completed state has an exact Bloch vector (cos χ cos 2φ, cos χ sin 2φ, sin χ), and the reciprocal pair becomes exact component-amplitude odds. The native complex carrier Ψ = H + iV is characterized exactly — an anisotropic real-linear squeeze of the circular phase with the counter-rotating decomposition Ψ = (i/8)(e^{2ix} + 3e^{−2ix}) — but without an independently selected physical embedding of its quadratures it determines no unique observable.

Spinor completion CONDITIONAL

The bare native carrier as unspecified physical embedding is Class B — its prediction set is undefined until a complete contract and dynamics are supplied.

§3 — Weak V−A helicity weights and the chirality-orientation obstruction

Now import the Standard Model's left-chiral charged-current projector P_L = (1−γ⁵)/2 — imported, not derived. For a massive electron the favored/wrong helicity probabilities are (1±β)/2, and their amplitude odds are exactly the reciprocal pair:

√(P_fav/P_wrong) = cxp,   √(P_wrong/P_fav) = crx

The equality is exact under the §1 kinematic contract. But the calculus is left–right symmetric: the mirror right-chiral orientation is equally available to the internal reciprocal structure until the Standard Model orientation is imported. Projection reconstructs the helicity-weight geometry of an imported chiral interaction; it does not derive why the interaction is left-chiral. That is the chirality-orientation obstruction, and it is retained, not repaired.

§4 — Beta decay, Coulomb dressing, and the two-angle bridge

Follow the emitted beta electron into the daughter nucleus's Coulomb field. Two independent physical angles are compulsory — not one. The continuum kinematic angle χ (sin χ = β, from §1) encodes the electron's mass-shell kinematics; the Coulomb structural angle a (sin a = Zα, cos a = √(1−(Zα)²)) encodes the daughter's Coulomb coupling. Identifying them would force β = Zα and collapse the continuous beta spectrum to a charge-selected velocity — the two-angle necessity theorem. The standard Coulomb parameter becomes their exact quotient,

ξ = Zα/β = sin a / sin χ = u(1+q²) / [q(1+u²)]

with u = sxp(a), q = sxp(χ). The baseline allowed phase-space measure becomes fully rational in q and the endpoint coordinate q₀. But the relativistic Fermi function keeps its non-elementary core exp(πξ)|Γ(λ+iξ)|²: the half-angle substitution rationalizes the kinematic and projective layers but does not generate the complex-Gamma Coulomb scattering dynamics. The structural angle a is the K=1 member of the Coulomb circle of the radial Dirac–Coulomb paper — the bound and continuum problems share the chart but use it differently (E_bound/m = cos a < 1 versus E_cont/m = sec χ > 1).

Two-angle Coulomb structure CONDITIONAL
  • a and χ are generically independent; a = χ would incorrectly force β = Zα.
  • ξ = sin a/sin χ exactly; phase space rational in (q, q₀).
  • The Fermi function, nuclear matrix elements, radiative and finite-size corrections, endpoint energy, and the nuclear scale R remain IMPORTs.
  • OBSTRUCTION The complex-Gamma factor lies outside the finite rational primitive closure — the special-function layer is imported dynamics, not derived.

§5 — Electromagnetic comparison: the adversarial audit

This section is deliberately adversarial. The question is not whether half-angle, Cayley, reciprocal, or doubled-angle forms occur in electromagnetism — they do, abundantly. The question is whether any occurrence survives the redundancies of electromagnetic description. The audit walks through nine witnesses and strips each one:

  • Jones polarization: z = e^{iφ} sxp(α) exactly — but phase φ and intensity scale A² are independent imported data (coherency/Stokes).
  • Duality: 2V = cos 2x, 4H = sin 2x give an exact SO(2) duality-rotation matrix — but constant duality is an accepted symmetry, not a new prediction.
  • Native carrier: Ψ = H+iV is an anisotropic squeeze with exact 1:3 counter-rotating decomposition — but direct H↔E, V↔B assignment fails an ordinary traveling wave; the potential-first contract A ∝ H recovers the standard wave after differentiation (a repair that reproduces Maxwell, not a new law).
  • Local duality: making the duality angle spacetime-dependent gives an SO(2) connection with zero curvature — pure gauge. The scalar alone does not defeat C0 by being made local.
  • Newman–Penrose: cxp/crx match reciprocal boost weights exactly — but those are tetrad-gauge weights, not new observables.
  • Null Maxwell spinor: the degree-two Veronese map [1:w:w²] — but only the null rank-one sector, not the generic field.
  • Two CP¹ factors: polarization CP¹ and propagation-direction CP¹ are independent state spaces; one real scalar cannot supply both.
  • Conformal dressing: in four dimensions the vacuum Maxwell equations are conformally invariant on 2-forms — a scalar conformal factor changes nothing in source-free propagation.
  • Helicity: electromagnetic helicity (massless spin-1) is not weak chirality (massive Dirac); the q=1/2 numerical coincidence (odds 3:1 in both sectors) promotes no cross-sector law.

The repeated verdict: exact coordinate correspondences, no surviving Projection-specific invariant.

OBSTRUCTION Invariant-survival audit. After removing projective, gauge, tetrad, duality, and conformal redundancies, every electromagnetic witness above is an exact correspondence inside known representation freedom. None establishes a Projection-specific invariant. C0 is strengthened, not weakened, by the redundancy analysis.
